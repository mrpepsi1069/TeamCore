What it does

Built for the people who run the team

The week-to-week work of a competitive roster — who plays, who's available, who's signed — without a spreadsheet. Every feature writes to the same database the dashboard reads.

01
Availability polls
Post one poll for game time or several time slots. Players tap what they can make and the results update live.
02
Depth charts
A full chart per team, stacked by position, with promote and demote when someone earns the starting job.
03
Lineups
Build and edit the week's lineup with positions, then post a formatted lineup straight to the channel.
04
Rosters
Track who is on the team, at what position and in what jersey; ask which teams any player is on.
05
Contracts
Track player contracts, mark them paid, and post every active deal as one clean embed.
06
Activity checks & broadcasts
Run an activity check to see who's still around, and DM everyone holding a role in one pass.
07
Records & stat channels
Log results and keep a win/loss record that updates itself in a voice channel — visible proof for recruits.
08
Awards & rings
Hand out awards and championship rings; players check their own with a single command.
09
Leagues & signups
When your team plays in a league, create it with signup buttons that assign the role automatically and see it in the console.
10
Moderation
Kick, ban, timeout and mute with full role-hierarchy checks — nobody moderates above their rank.
11
Server setup
Map channels and roles to permission tiers once, and the bot fits the server you already have.
